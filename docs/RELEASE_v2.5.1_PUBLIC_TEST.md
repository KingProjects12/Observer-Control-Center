# Observer 2.5.1 — Curated themes

The theme gallery now contains 26 themes: Signal, Black Liquid Glass and the 24 Design Lab presets (Studio, Worlds and Lab). Removed 16 earlier experimental and seasonal presets. Preferences pointing to removed themes safely fall back to Black Liquid Glass. Seasonal effects are hidden until a future seasonal collection; the animation infrastructure remains for future development. Search, live theme selection and the retained palettes are unchanged. No networking, report processing or updater changes.

# Observer 2.5.1 — Отбор тем

Оставлены Signal, Black Liquid Glass и 24 темы Design Lab. Удалены 16 прежних тем. При запуске с удалённой темой применяется Black Liquid Glass. Сезонные эффекты временно скрыты до новой коллекции. Сетевое ядро, отчёты и обновлятор не изменены.
